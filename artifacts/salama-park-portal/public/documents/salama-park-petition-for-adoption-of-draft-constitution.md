# PETITION FOR THE ADOPTION OF THE ENTIRE DRAFT CONSTITUTION AND THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEW REGISTER FOR ONLINE VOTING

**PETITION AND WRITTEN RESOLUTION OF THE RESIDENTS OF SALAMA PARK**

## Purpose
This petition seeks the adoption of the entire Draft Constitution of the Salama Park Residents Association (SPRA), including Article 15 (Transitional and Founding Provisions), and the establishment of a new register of members based on verified signatures to serve as the voter roll for an online adoption vote and first election.

## Key Points
1. **Adopt the entire Draft Constitution** - including all proposed amendments, Article 5A (Street Representatives), Article 5B (External Representation and Secondment), Article 7A (nominations/elections), new Vice positions and Publicity Secretary, renumbering, and Article 15.
2. **New register of members** - verified signatures form the register for quorum, thresholds, eligibility, and the voter roll for the first online election.
3. **Online voting** - adoption and first elections to be conducted by an approved online voting system that ensures one vote per member, secret ballot, receipt codes, verifiability, and real-time turnout monitoring.
4. **Founding period** - Article 15 suspends the "fully paid-up" requirement until a subscription schedule is adopted; no subscription is required to sign, vote, stand, or be elected during the founding period.
5. **Thresholds** - adoption requires signatures of not less than two-thirds (2/3) of members on the register; a minimum of 25% constitutes quorum for considering the Constitution.
6. **Verification** - conducted by an independent Returning Officer with two scrutineers (woman and youth); results published and records preserved.

## Signing
By signing, residents consent to adopting the entire Draft Constitution (including Article 15), agree to be entered in the new register, and understand no subscription is payable to sign. Signatures may be withdrawn in writing before closing date.

## Implementation Timeline (summary)
1. Appoint Returning Officer & scrutineers
2. Circulate petition and Constitution
3. Collect signatures (minimum 21-day window recommended)
4. Verify and publish register
5. Approve online voting system and conduct vote/elections
6. Table subscription schedule within 30 days of adoption (Art. 15.5)
